About Me

Professional Background

My background began in education, where I spent eight years designing curriculum and creating engaging learning experiences for students. I later transitioned into instructional design, bringing that learner-centered mindset into corporate training. Today, I design e-learning courses, videos, job aids, and other learning resources, working closely with SMEs to turn complex information into clear, practical learning experiences.

Career Aspirations & Goals

My goal is to design learning experiences that help people learn more effectively and turn what they learn into action. I want to create clear and practical learning that helps learners build skills, solve problems, and perform with confidence. As I grow in my career, I hope to explore new design approaches and technologies that make learning easier to apply in real-world situations.

Personal Interests

I call North Carolina home, where I live with my husband and our little boy. When I'm not designing learning experiences, you’ll probably find me planning our next trip, skiing, or trying a new recipe in the kitchen. That same curiosity keeps me exploring new ideas and possibilities in my work.
